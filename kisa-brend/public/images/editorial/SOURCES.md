# Editorial photographs

These assets are image-region crops of the design PNGs supplied by the user:

- `О нас — desktop.png`, `О нас — планшет.png`, `О нас — мобильная.png`
- `Новости — desktop.png`, `Новости — планшет.png`, `Новости — мобильная.png`

The `-mobile` and `-tablet` files preserve the distinct photographs in each
reference. `EditorialImage` selects them with `<picture>` at 640 and 1100 px.
No page text or interactive controls are embedded in these crops.
